ExCluster privacy policy (Android app)

ExCluster shows speed, a virtual RPM, trips, clock and a few gauges on a phone used as an instrument cluster. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not share data with anyone. Everything below stays on the phone unless the table says otherwise.

Data What for Leaves the phone? Stored?
Precise location (GPS) Speed, trip distance, GPS state; the current position's weather Only for the weather request: latitude and longitude rounded to 3 decimals (about 100 m) go to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) Not stored (see "Drive log" for the optional route)
Motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope) G gauge, estimating speed through tunnels, noticing that the phone is being handled No Not stored (only in the optional drive log)
Ambient light sensor Adjusts the screen brightness automatically (settings: "Screen brightness: Auto") No Not stored (only in the optional drive log)
Battery level and temperature of the phone Battery gauge, drive log No Not stored (only in the optional drive log)
Car data through Android Auto, only while the "Android Auto" switch in the settings is on (off by default; the car data permissions are asked when you turn it on) (speed, displayed speed, battery level and range, odometer, car model, charging state, tolls card state, car sensors) Shown on the car screen so you can see what the car provides, and on the phone's gauge (odometer, battery level, range) No Written to a local file carprobe.csv on the phone (capped at 8 MB) while the car app runs; it contains no coordinates. The last odometer, battery level and range are kept in the app's settings on the phone; the car speed is used as a back-up when GPS is lost
"My sound" (optional): an audio file you pick yourself Plays a short looped part of it, changed by speed and acceleration No. The app reads the file you choose and never uploads it Only the 5 to 10 second part you choose is kept, in the app's private storage (not the whole file); you can delete it in the app, and it is excluded from backups and device transfers
Spoken speed (optional, off by default) Says your current speed every few minutes while you drive The sentence (for example "Current speed 80 kilometers per hour") is handed to the text-to-speech engine installed on the phone; the app itself sends nothing. If that engine uses an online voice, its own privacy policy applies No
Settings, trip A / B distances, learned phone mount direction Keep your choices No On the phone
Drive log (hidden debug mode, off by default) Analysing speed accuracy Only if you export it yourself (share sheet) On the phone: speed, sensor values (including the ambient light), satellite counts and timings. Latitude / longitude are written only if you switch on "Record route" (off by default); the export can leave them out. Logs older than the chosen number of days (default 14) are deleted automatically
  • Debug logs, the car-data file and the "My sound" clip are excluded from Android backups and device transfers.
  • You can turn location off in the phone's settings at any time; the app then cannot show speed. Delete the app (or its data) to remove everything it stored; the drive log screen can also delete sessions.
  • Weather: the request contains only the rounded coordinates, no identifier. Open-Meteo's own policy applies to it.
  • Children: the app is not directed at children.
  • Contact: wavullet@gmail.com

Effective date: 2026-11-15
