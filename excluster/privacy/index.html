ExCluster privacy policy (Android app)

ExCluster shows speed, a virtual RPM, trips, clock and a few gauges on a phone used as an instrument cluster. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not share data with anyone. Everything below stays on the phone unless the table says otherwise.

Data What for Leaves the phone? Stored?
Precise location (GPS) Speed, trip distance, GPS state; the current position's weather Only for the weather request: latitude and longitude rounded to 3 decimals (about 100 m) go to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) Not stored, except by the optional "Record drive routes" and the hidden debug log (below)
Motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope) G gauge, estimating speed through tunnels, noticing that the phone is being handled No Not stored (only in the optional debug log)
Ambient light sensor Adjusts the screen brightness automatically (settings: "Screen brightness: Auto") No Not stored (only in the optional debug log)
Battery level and temperature of the phone Battery gauge, debug log No Not stored (only in the optional debug log)
Car data through Android Auto, only while the "Android Auto" switch in the settings is on (off by default; the car data permissions are asked when you turn it on) (speed, displayed speed, battery level and range, odometer, car model, charging state, tolls card state, car sensors) Shown on the car screen so you can see what the car provides, and on the phone's gauge (odometer, battery level, range) No Written to a local file carprobe.csv on the phone (capped at 8 MB) while the car app runs; it contains no coordinates. The last odometer, battery level and range are kept in the app's settings on the phone; the car speed is used as a back-up when GPS is lost
"My sound" (optional): an audio file you pick yourself Plays a short looped part of it, changed by speed and acceleration No. The app reads the file you choose and never uploads it Only the 5 to 10 second part you choose is kept, in the app's private storage (not the whole file); you can delete it in the app, and it is excluded from backups and device transfers
Spoken warnings (overheat warning, "Check the voice" in the settings) Says a warning when the phone gets hot The sentence (for example "The phone is very hot") is handed to the text-to-speech engine installed on the phone; the app itself sends nothing. If that engine uses an online voice, its own privacy policy applies No
"Copy test info" (a button in the settings, for people who test the app) Copies the app version, the phone's make, model and Android version, the theme / orientation / HUD setting as plain text, so a tester can paste it into a feedback form No. It is copied to the phone's clipboard only when you press the button; the app never sends or stores it. It contains no location, device ID, account or name; any app that can read the clipboard (the system shows a notice on newer Android versions) can see it until you copy something else No
Settings, trip A / B distances, learned phone mount direction Keep your choices No On the phone
"Record drive routes" (Detailed settings, off by default) Lists your trips (distance, time, highest and average speed) and lets you open a trip in a map app Only if you share trips yourself (share sheet, GPX or KML file, or a zip of several trips); the app asks first and says the file holds the whole route, start and end included On the phone only: the route of each trip (latitude, longitude, time and speed, one point every few seconds while moving). Also on the phone only: the list of trips already exported (trip file names). Neither is in the phone's backup or device transfer. Trips older than the chosen number of days (default 30) are deleted automatically, and you can delete one or all in the app
Debug log (hidden debug mode, off by default) Analysing speed accuracy Only if you export it yourself (share sheet) On the phone: speed, sensor values (including the ambient light and the air pressure), satellite counts and timings. Latitude / longitude are written only if you switch on "Location in debug logs" in the hidden menu (off by default; separate from "Record drive routes" above); the export can leave them out. Logs older than the chosen number of days (default 14) are deleted automatically
  • Recorded drive routes, debug logs, the car-data file and the "My sound" clip are excluded from Android backups and device transfers.
  • You can turn location off in the phone's settings at any time; the app then cannot show speed. Delete the app (or its data) to remove everything it stored; the route list and the debug log screen can also delete trips and sessions.
  • Weather: the request contains only the rounded coordinates, no identifier. Open-Meteo's own policy applies to it.
  • Children: the app is not directed at children.
